## Train all eye movements

Training mode goes further than simply holding the eyes in one position. The image moves in controlled patterns that make the eyes move outward, rotate, and change position in different combinations, so more of the eye-muscle system takes part in the exercise.

This means the training is not limited to one movement or one pair of muscles. Different patterns can work the muscles that move the eyes left and right, up and down, and rotate them. The result is a more complete form of eye-muscle training than static stereo viewing alone.

The goal is to improve flexibility, coordination, and the usable range of eye movement with short, repeatable sessions. Instead of learning complicated exercises or trying to consciously control individual muscles, you can simply follow the visual pattern while Stereo Reader provides the movement.
